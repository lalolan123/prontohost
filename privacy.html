Privacy

Last updated October 2026. Pronto Host is a business based in Maple, Ontario. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

When you contact us

If you fill in our form or call us, we keep your name, restaurant, phone number and message so we can call you back about Pronto Host. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

When you call a restaurant that uses Pronto Host

We answer on behalf of that restaurant. To take your booking, order or message, the call is transcribed and may be recorded, and you're told this at the start of the call. The details you give (your name, number, booking or order, any notes such as allergies) go to the restaurant you called. The restaurant decides how long to keep them. We use them only to provide the service to that restaurant.

When you try a demo restaurant on this website

The demo restaurants are made up. When you press a Talk button, your browser asks to use your microphone, and your voice goes to our voice provider, ElevenLabs, so the demo host can answer. The demo call is transcribed and may be recorded, and is deleted after 90 days. Please don't give real personal details to a demo restaurant. This website also loads fonts from Google and the demo's code from jsDelivr, which see your internet address when your browser fetches them.

Where it's stored

We use service providers to run the phone line, the voice host and this website. Some of them store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessed under local law. We choose providers that protect data under contract.

How long we keep it

Call recordings and transcripts are deleted after 90 days unless the restaurant asks us to keep them longer or shorter. Contact form details are kept while we're in touch with you, and deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Text or call Matteo at (647) 771-8799, or write to Pronto Host, Maple, Ontario. Matteo Lan is responsible for privacy at Pronto Host and will answer within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.
