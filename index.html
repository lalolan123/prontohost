Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will callers know it's not a person?

Yes. The greeting says it's your restaurant's AI host, and if anyone asks, it says so plainly. Most callers care that someone picked up and got it right.

Can a caller reach a real person?

Yes. If a caller asks for a person, the host takes their name and number and texts the manager right away to call back.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your phone company forwards calls to Pronto Host when the line is busy, after a set number of rings, or after hours. Bell, Rogers and most business lines can do this, and we help you set it up.

What if it can't answer something?

It takes a message with the caller's name and number and texts it to you right away, so someone can call back.

How does it handle allergies?

Carefully. It tells callers what's on your menu sheet, writes down every allergy, and flags it for your staff. It never tells a caller a dish is safe for their allergy. That stays with your kitchen.

Are calls recorded? What about privacy?

Callers are told at the start that the call may be recorded to get their order right. Recordings and caller details belong to your restaurant, are used only to serve your callers, and are deleted on request.

What happens when I change the menu or hours?

Text or email Matteo. Changes are usually live the same day, and holiday hours are included.

What if I go over my minutes?

Nothing gets cut off. We'll tell you, and if it happens two months in a row we'll suggest the plan that fits.

Is there a contract?

No. After your free month it's month to month. Cancel with one text or email.
